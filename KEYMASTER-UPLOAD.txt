FD-robberies - Cfx.re Asset Escrow upload guide
================================================

1. Keep this source package as a private, unencrypted backup.
2. Upload the ZIP from the FIVE DEV owner account at https://portal.cfx.re/.
3. Open Created Assets, choose Create an asset, and name it FD-robberies.
4. Upload the ZIP and wait for Cfx.re processing.
5. Download and test the encrypted build on an entitled server.
6. For sales, attach this FiveM Asset to the matching Tebex package.

Protection layout
-----------------
- client.lua and server.lua: protected by Asset Escrow.
- config.lua: editable customer configuration.
- html/: shipped as readable NUI files because NUI encryption is unsupported.
- SQL, item definitions, documentation and license: readable.

Important
---------
- Never upload your only source copy.
- Never add client.lua or server.lua to escrow_ignore.
- Encryption is performed by Cfx.re after upload, not locally.
